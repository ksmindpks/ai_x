 Major Problems and Improvements of P2P Loan Business1. Lack of regulatory and supervisory frameworkP2P loans were incorporated into the system in 2020 with the enactment of the Online Investment-linked Financial Business Act, but the supervisory system is still insufficient.,The delinquency rate rose from 3.05% in November 2022 to 3.60% in October 2023, and some point out that the poor management system is insufficient.2. Insufficient protection of investorsP2P investments are not subject to depositor protection and are likely to be lost when investing in high-risk products such as real estate PFs. Investor protection devices are weak and risk notices are insufficient.3. The issue of pre-financing practicesSome platforms use a method of recruiting investors after pre-financing, which is feared to infringe on investor rights. Financial authorities have banned it, but the industry continues to clash, claiming a realistic need.4. Lack of investment limits and disclosuresThe investment limit is controversial between market activation and investor protection, and it is difficult to judge investment due to lack of disclosure information related to products.5. Inadequate management systemDespite the upgrading of credit ratings, there is a risk of delinquency and insolvency due to the presence of a portion of low-credit borrowers. In the past, several P2P companies' bankruptcy cases have greatly undermined investor confidence.6. Warning of foreign casesIn China, the P2P market has grown rapidly in the absence of regulations, and the industry has collapsed due to massive insolvency and fraud, suggesting the need for investor protection and strong regulation.improvement directionStrengthen supervision: Establish a supervisory system centered on investor protection, require platform financial soundness and internal control checksExpand protection: Introduce loss minimization devices, increase product risk noticesImproving operational transparency: stipulating a ban on pre-emptive loans, clarifying platform rolesStrengthening overdue response: elaborating credit rating, introducing a system to collect bad debtsInvestor training: Provide investment risk information, systematize damage prevention guidanceReflecting the Global Case: Balancing Industry Self-Regulation with Government Oversight NeedsConclusion 

 P2P loans have potential as a new financial service, but without institutional supplementation, investor damage can be repeated. It is urgent to establish effective regulations and protection devices.
